Rounding steps: ₱0.01 · ₱0.10 · ₱1 · ₱10 (Excel ROUND / ROUNDUP with 2, 1, 0, −1). Base DP uses nearest; Threshold rounds up. Special price for rebate SKUs = ROUNDUP((NDP − rebate) × 1.12 ÷ (1 − threshold margin)) while rebate units are left. Under the category's Limited below margin without the rebate (blank = 5%) → Limited Qty Price. New = Auto by date: released or added to the OPG within the last 90 days (change in Admin → Settings). Pick New or Existing on a SKU to override. The OPG date fills itself when you upload the HP OPG (Admin → Data uploads); SKUs missing from the latest OPG show EOL until they come back.
