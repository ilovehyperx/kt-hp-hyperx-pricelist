Admin: everything here, the pricelist editor, every dealer page, cost and margins. Sales: the sales special-price page, plus any dealer pages they're assigned to. No cost or margin. Channel sales (CSO): only the dealer pages they're assigned to. Adding an email to a dealer page adds it here as a CSO automatically. Sales assistant (CSA): same as Sales — special prices, all dealer pages, quotations. HP lead: only dealer pages they're assigned to, view only. Dealer account: only its own dealer page, read-only, stock shown as levels; set it as a viewer on that dealer page. CSO manager: the sales special-price page and every active dealer page, view only (no cost, margin or editing). Promote to Admin if they should edit. The owner account () always stays an active admin, and you can't remove your own admin access from here.
